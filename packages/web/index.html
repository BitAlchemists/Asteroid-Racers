Controls

← → turn, ↑ thrust (or A D W), 1 toggles the interface, Esc opens the game menu (restart, change track, back to this page). Fly into the green start pad to join the race; click it (or “Leave race” in multiplayer) to leave.

On a phone: drag anywhere on the left half to steer (the ship turns to point where you drag), hold anywhere on the right half to thrust. “☰ Menu” at the top left opens the game menu.
