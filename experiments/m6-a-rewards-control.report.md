**Verdicts.**
- By the decision rule (exam checkpoints): **no arm wins**.
- The experiment is underpowered: the detectable effect at 10 seeds is 0.06–0.19, against a minEffect
  of 0.03.
- Exam crashes: only the residual controller differs clearly, −415 [−705, −106], 8 of 10 seeds
  better. No arm triggers the crash veto.

**What the secondary data show.**
- **3.4 residual controller:** the strongest arm.
  - Exam crashes drop by about a third, and spin falls to 0.28.
  - It is the only setup that brakes: Stop 95 %, against 0 % for every other arm.
  - Corridor skill doubles (38 %) and Field rises to 72 %.
  - Seed 5 is the exception: its exam checkpoints fell by 0.555. That one seed widens the CI; worth
    a look before adoption.
- **2.1 progress:** fixes the multi-target reward trap, as predicted.
  - RaceLegs goes from 88 % to 99 % with crashes 2.2 → 0.8, Chain from 76 % to 91 %, Rock1 from 64 % to
    83 %.
  - The exam is unchanged: the baseline trains on single-target episodes, where the trap cannot bite.
- **2.8 rotation cost:** reduces spinning (0.45 at 20, 0.30 at 50) without hurting the exam.
  - At 20, TurnAndGo goes to 88 % and Rock2 to 65 %.
  - 50 costs a little exam performance.
- **2.2:** crash 1000 is the worst arm (more crashes, fewer checkpoints). Miss 3000 is neutral.

**Recommendation for the decision point.**
1. **Adopt 3.4 residual 0.5** as the new default, by judgement as with E4b. Grounds: clearly fewer
   crashes, far better skills, spinning mostly gone, exam not worse. Check seed 5's collapse first.
2. **Adopt 2.1 progress 3.** It is the prerequisite for batch B's multi-target curriculum (2.6) and
   for 3.1 lookahead. The skills show the trap fix.
3. **Optional: rotation 20.** Spinning is already mostly gone with the residual controller, so
   test it in the follow-up rather than adopting it now.
4. **Drop** crash 1000 and miss 3000.
5. **Follow-up before batch B**, like E4b: residual + progress together, and residual + progress +
   rotation 20, against the baseline, ideally at 20 seeds (power).
6. **For batch B:** run the curriculum experiments on the combined baseline. With the residual
   controller already braking and turning, 2.6's tiered levels should climb faster.
