### What the arms show

- **The curriculum is not the bottleneck on this baseline.** With the residual controller every
  seed climbs M4's levels at the minimum pace (one level per 3-generation streak) and reaches the
  last level at g13–17; m5tiers' five levels take g13–18. So:
  - **2.5 plateau** never fires (patience 10 is longer than a 3-generation climb). The runs are
    identical to the baseline on every metric; the stateHash differs only because the plateau
    state is part of it.
  - **2.6 m5tiers** spends 13–18 generations on other arenas and then flies the same task: no
    difference.
- **2.4 keepEasy** is the only arm with a clear positive effect (+1.4, 16 of 20 seeds), below the
  3-point minimum. It acts on the final level (a quarter of each arena's episodes from easier
  levels), and its exam3 curve still rises at g150. The gain is in easy, medium and the goal races.
  No veto.
- **2.6 m7gen** learns caution: crashes per race drop from 35.6 to 9.0 and medium tracks improve
  (+5.8, 18 of 20 seeds). But it finishes fewer races, loses on easy tracks (−15), hand-made tracks
  and the race-legs and rock skills, and freezes often (below). Its exam3 still rises at g150
  (45.0 → 47.4). It reaches its last level at g10–121 (median g23). It trains on the exam's track
  distribution and is still worse, so the generated curriculum in its S5 form is no shortcut.
- **2.7 heldOutCurrent:** training is identical to the baseline: the same training score every
  generation, exam3 and exam v2 equal at every exam, even seed 5's g150 freeze repeats exactly.
  The stateHash differs from g1, though. The extra held-out flight changes a state the hash covers
  (most likely the server generator's), so Evo Lab's gate would treat this measurement-only switch
  as a different run. The measurement question can't be answered on this baseline: the two
  held-out metrics differ only in the 11–16 generations before the last level, and the first exam
  is at g25. Over the exam generations both correlate identically with exam3 (median ρ 0.6).

### Collapses and seed 5

Screen: exam v2 checkpoints below 0.6 at any exam generation; a freeze has few crashes, a crash
loop many.

- **Seed 5 at g150** freezes in heldOutCurrent (train-669: 0.28, exam3 42.9, the same numbers as
  train-549, because training is identical) and, by construction, in plateau (train-609). It does
  **not** freeze in keepEasy (train-589: 0.955), m5tiers (train-629: 0.90) or m7gen (train-649:
  0.82). The g150 freeze follows the exact lineage, not the seed.
- **keepEasy:** one freeze (train-588 g100). **m5tiers:** one crash loop (train-635 g100).
  **heldOutCurrent:** the baseline's drops, repeated.
- **m7gen:** 27 drops in 20 runs, most of them freezes, also at g150 (train-647 0.54, train-653
  0.36, train-662 0.46). The generated curriculum makes freezing much more common.

### Recommendation (not signed off; the decisions are the user's)

1. **No arm wins by the rule.** Nothing goes into the default config.
