- **Plateau:** from g150 to g300 the exam IQM does not improve. Checkpoints go 0.89 → 0.86 and
  finished 0.86 → 0.82, while held-out success rises slightly (0.65 → 0.69).
- **Seed 7's g300 exam score of 1.15M** is an outlier: 54 % finished, and the score is inflated by
  the distance-left term (very likely a flight that leaves the track far behind). This is why the
  IQM is the summary to read.
- **No collapse:** no seed drops below 10 % of checkpoints after being above 50 %.
- **Levels:** 8 of 10 seeds reach the full task (level 4); seed 1 ends on level 2, seed 10 on
  level 1.
- **Speed:** median 12,700 ticks/s per run for g151–300, with up to 20 runs in parallel.
- **Best model:** seed 8, train-327 at g300. Exam v2 57,808, 94.5 % of checkpoints, 91 % of races
  finished, 711 crashes (about 7 per race). An exam-only re-run (exam-7, `--input-run train-327`,
  102 races) reproduced these numbers exactly.
- **Observation from the replay** (inputs panel, before the viewer check was cancelled): in
  rock-in-line episodes the turn output stays at −1 the whole time, so the ship spins at maximum
  rate. It pulses thrust (0 ↔ 1) when facing roughly towards the target, and `lineCheck.near`
  rises while it approaches a rock in line. It is a "spin and pulse" controller, not a steering
  one.
